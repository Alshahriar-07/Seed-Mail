Secure Email Campaign Management

Sign in to read your Gmail inbox, compose and send email from your own account, manage recipients and templates, and run personalised campaigns with real progress and a complete submission history.

Signed-in features require an account. Mail is read and sent through Google's official Gmail API from your own account — Seed Code Mail never asks for your Gmail password. Email templates stay in this browser, and a campaign saves a snapshot to your own account row (protected by row level security) so the send worker can deliver it.
